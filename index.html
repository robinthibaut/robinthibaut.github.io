Robin Thibaut

Head of Agentic Systems R&D at Zanskar

Curriculum vitae (PDF)

As Head of Agentic Systems R&D at Zanskar, I lead research on durable AI agent systems for long-running scientific, technical, and operational work. My earlier work was in computational geoscience, spanning geothermal exploration, hydrologic modeling, geophysical methods, scientific software, field evidence, and uncertainty.

Core Expertise

Agent systems and evaluation · Geothermal exploration · Hydrogeological and geophysical modeling · Bayesian and machine-learning methods for uncertainty · Scientific software and reproducible field data
